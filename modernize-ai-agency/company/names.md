## Top 3

1. ParityProof - best fit: the name is the guarantee (100% behavioral parity, proven), instantly clear to a CTO and easy to spell. No company or trademark of that exact name found. parityproof.ai available and parityproof.io available at USD 40; the .com belongs to someone else. Watch the crowded 'Parity' prefix (US PARITY Reg. 5754173 in class 42; Parity Technologies Pvt Ltd in Bangalore). Suggested legal name: 'ParityProof Technologies (OPC) Private Limited'.
2. Paritycraft - safest domains: paritycraft.com (USD 16) and paritycraft.ai both available. No company or trademark of that name found. Credible engineering tone, slightly softer than 'proof'. Same 'Parity'-prefix caveat. Suggested legal name: 'Paritycraft Technologies (OPC) Private Limited'.
3. Equivalis - most distinctive: an invented word, so it is the strongest to protect as a trademark. No company or trademark conflicts found; equivalis.ai available (the .com is registered by someone). Its meaning (equivalence) suits enterprise buyers but needs a tagline. Suggested legal name: 'Equivalis Technologies (OPC) Private Limited'.

## Method

1) I read the repo README and the CTO outbound pitch to understand the offer: a $50k 14-day pilot, a $100k/month retainer, and a 100% parity guarantee proven by a characterization harness and dual-engine replay. The current working name is 'LegacyModern AI'. 2) I generated ideas with the Shopify generate-business-names tool plus my own parity/proof/migration names. I dropped names close to large-firm marks (e.g. ProofPoint, Isomorphic, Heirloom, Cobalt, Cutover), then shortlisted 15 from about 40 domain checks. 3) Live domain checks used Shopify generate-domain-names on 2026-10-13. I passed each exact domain as the query: .com first, then .ai, then .io only where both were taken. Every result is in /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-AI-Agency-/14c05ad1-c091-5598-becd-ffaad63ec49e/scratchpad/company_build/names/domain_checks.tsv. Prices are recorded exactly as returned; the tool returned no price for any .ai domain. 4) Conflict checks: a general web search for each name; searches of MCA-mirror sites (zaubacorp.com, tofler.in, thecompanycheck.com, instafinancials.com, falconebiz.com, quickcompany.in); an Exa company-category search; Exa fetches of the taken .com sites to see who uses them; and trademark aggregator searches (uspto.report, Trademarkia, Justia). 5) MCA rules: I verified the current text of rules 8, 8A and 8B of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules 2014, as substituted by G.S.R. 357(E) of 10 May 2019, from indiankanoon, aubsp and ziplaw, plus the OPC name format from the SPICe instruction kit. I applied rule 8(2) (disregarded matters), 8A(1)(b), (h), (n), (s) and 8B to each name. I also checked ISO's official name-use policy because of the 'Iso-' candidates. 6) Scored 1-10 on: fit with the parity/proof offer and credibility with CTOs; ease of spelling; distinctiveness and trademark strength; domain availability; existing-company or trademark conflicts; and MCA risk. ## Caveats

- Before filing, final MCA name approval must come through SPICe+ Part A or RUN on the MCA portal; only the RoC decides. Separately, run an IP India public trademark search (at least classes 42, 35 and 9) for the chosen name. Ideally also search the main export markets: USPTO (US), UKIPO (UK), CIPO (Canada), IP Australia and IPOS (Singapore). None of this research replaces those searches or a trademark attorney's clearance opinion.
- Domain availability was a point-in-time check on 2026-10-13 and holds nothing; the domains could be taken at any moment. Prices are first-term quotes only, and renewals are often higher. The tool returned no price for any .ai domain, so .ai cost is unconfirmed.
- For the taken .com domains (parityproof.com, twinrun.com, equivalis.com, codeparity.com, legacymodern.com, exactport.com), whether they are parked, for sale or in active use is unconfirmed. WebFetch could not resolve DNS from this sandbox, curl was blocked by the proxy, and Exa returned empty content or timed out.
- MCA-mirror sites (Zauba Corp, Tofler, TheCompanyCheck and others) are not authoritative and may lag. They do not show names currently reserved under rule 9, LLP names or pending applications, and an empty search there does not prove a name is available.
- Rule 8A(1)(b) blocks only names that include a trademark registered in the same class without the owner's consent. Separately from MCA approval, an earlier user (registered or not) can still bring a passing-off claim.
- The 'Parity' prefix is crowded in software: Parity Technologies Ltd (UK), Parity Technologies Pvt Ltd (Bangalore), ParityQC, Paritybit Security, ParityFactory, and the US mark PARITY Reg. 5754173 in classes 36/42 owned by Parity Labs LLC. The current status of that registration was not checked against the USPTO itself. Counsel should assess this for ParityProof and Paritycraft.
- ISO's policy says not to use 'ISO' in company or domain names and calls its marks well-known (https://www.iso.org/iso-name-and-logo.html). This is why Isoparity and Isomodern were marked down despite having available .com domains.
- OPC facts verified: only an Indian-citizen natural person, resident or not, may form an OPC (rule 3); the name format is 'ABC (OPC) Private Limited' per the SPICe instruction kit; and 'One Person Company' must appear in brackets below the name wherever it is printed (s.12(3) proviso, per a secondary source). Rule 8A(2) also requires a declaration of whether the name has been used in another business in the last five years.
- Not researched, so unconfirmed: whether the RoC queries 'AI' in a legal company name, the name-reservation validity period, and MCA filing fees.
- No domain was purchased and no name was reserved or registered. No email, form, filing or contact with any person or company was made.
